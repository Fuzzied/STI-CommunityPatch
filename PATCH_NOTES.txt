Space Travel Idle: Community Patch 0.1
======================================

For Space Travel Idle v0.35.43, the community version. Windows and Mac.

Some things below are described vaguely on purpose, so nothing is spoiled
before you reach it. The cards in the game explain themselves when you get
there.


Before you start
----------------

- Installing: close the game, then run the setup. The READ ME FIRST file
  next to it walks you through it.
  Win: "Setup Space Travel Idle Mod.bat"
  Mac: "Setup Space Travel Idle Mod.command"
- Everything is optional. All 44 patches are on when the menu opens.
  Press Enter to take them all, or c to pick.
- Your saves are never touched by the setup.
- Taking it off: run the setup again and choose r. Every original game
  file goes back.
- After a Steam update or file check, run the setup again. Steam puts the
  original files back, nothing breaks.


The Balancer
------------

I tuned this patch with a second mod on: the Unlocker by Berserker, which I
call the Balancer. It lets you set the game's bonuses in a settings file.

- The setup installs it for you, with the settings I used. Both lines are
  on when the menu opens.
- Without it and my settings, I lost every high level fight, trips took
  twice as long, and Big Bang paid nothing unless I beat my best score.
  You also get about a third of the drops and half the research.
- Rather play the plain game? Untick "Balancer". Version 0.2 will be
  balanced for that too.
- Already have it? Your own copy and settings are kept aside. Your settings
  come back if you untick mine, your copy comes back when you choose r.
- Not my work. Shared under its MIT licence, which the setup copies next to
  it. Source: https://github.com/Berserker66/STIU


Offline changes
---------------

The base game has no offline limit. Be gone a month and you are credited the
whole month.

This patch adds one:

- One day for free. Anything past that is not credited.
- Two cheap lines in the Big Bang tree stretch it, up to sixteen weeks.
  They were asked for as upgrades that add offline days, and they needed a
  limit to add to.
- Offline boss kills follow the same limit.
- Rather have no limit? Untick "Offline time limit" in the installer.
  You are back to the base game's unlimited offline time.
  - The two Big Bang lines lock, since there is no limit for them to raise.
  - Levels you already own stay, and you can take them back down for a
    refund.
  - You can also do it by hand: in the game folder, open
    BepInEx\config\sti.community.offlinefix.cfg and set
    CapOfflineTime = false


Changes in the mod
------------------

Bug fixes

- Saves that fail halfway and drop you into the tutorial with 0 dark matter.
- Three destinations you cannot travel to, and two you can never reach.
- A travel research that does nothing.
- Production and dead ends on the far planets.
- Three late researches that can never unlock.
- Some fights freeze on the enemy's turn.
- Cards that can never be levelled or stop partway up, and one that fell
  out of the drop tables.
- The Star Essence upgrade only counted essence earned after you bought it.
  It now counts what you already hold.
- Travel achievements reset with a Big Bang. They now go by the furthest you
  have ever been.
- Text showing as raw code words instead of names.
- Filters forget what you set them to when you load.
- Lists jump back to the top every time a research completes.
- The game slows to a crawl and stops taking clicks once your research runs
  fast.
- Battle: health buffs now fill your health too, and a bigger hand fits on
  screen.
- The developer error console is hidden.

Balance

- Damage: a hit used to be attack minus defense, so it was either a
  one-shot or exactly 0. Defense now always takes off a share and never
  makes you immortal. Applies to enemies too.
- Three challenge zones that ended up hundreds of times stronger than
  anything after them now scale so you can beat them about one planet later
  than where you find them.
- Three battle cards that stopped scaling now grow with your cards, and a
  fourth that nobody could use is reworked.
- Costs retuned: Mars water and soil, two late game upgrades, and one
  planet that cost more than the one after it.

New content

- More Big Bang upgrades, and the lines you already have go further. Most
  of the new ones unlock later in the game.
- One more permanent card slot, late game.
- A second level of an early research, so your whole deck is in your hand
  every fight.
- A new card to find.
- Cargo you can actually fly with. Your hull weighs 100 all game, and a
  full hold weighs millions, later billions. So a full hold makes a journey
  take thousands of times as long, and it gets worse every time you raise
  your storage. Nothing in the game tells you. The fix is in the Big Bang
  tree.
- Buildings that keep paying after you leave a planet.
- Icons for the new Big Bang upgrades, and artwork for cards that never had
  any.

Quality of life

- Auto start: research, buildings and collectors start by themselves when
  you land, set off or Big Bang, even if the game was closed.
- Loadouts: more saved decks and permanent sets, one more per planet you
  reach, up to 10 each. Loading one puts on the best copy you own of each
  card.
- Keyboard shortcuts for the things you repeat all evening.
- Alchemy forge: a MAX button, a DE-LEVEL switch that splits merged cards
  back down when a recipe is short, and a stop once a recipe has made a
  Perfect card, so you do not waste resources chasing a better roll.
- Collectors fill the tank right to the top, instead of stopping up to a
  whole bar short.
- Tooltips: choose how long they stay on screen.
- Battles: the discard zone is gone.
- The Big Bang window: the dark matter total no longer covers an
  upgrade.
- The game window opens where you last left it (Windows, windowed mode).

Performance

- The stutter while your fleet fights is gone. Every kill made the game
  work out every drop chance from scratch, and cleaning up after that was
  the hitch you saw. Late in the game it cost a lot of frame rate and let
  the game clock fall behind. The answers are the same as before, just
  remembered.


Known issues
------------

- The Mac side has not been tested on a real Mac yet. It is written
  against what the Mac build should look like. If you are on a Mac and try
  it, please say how it went, good or bad. Nothing there can hurt your save.


Found a problem?
----------------

Tell Fuzzied on Discord. Even "it looked odd for a second" helps.

It saves a round of questions if you include:

- The build number the setup prints at the top of its window.
- Two log files:
  - LogOutput.log, in the BepInEx folder inside the game folder.
  - Player.log, which is here:
    Win: %userprofile%\AppData\LocalLow\Ayatsuji\SpaceTravelIdle
    Mac: ~/Library/Logs/Ayatsuji/SpaceTravelIdle
